Projects

Spectrophotometer

The project presents the design, fabrication and validation of a low-cost, portable spectrophotometer for assessing MTV contamination. The design is described and the accuracy and reliability of the instrument is evaluated, offering an accessible and effective solution to evaluate the quality of viral transport media and, therefore, the accuracy of COVID-19 tests. In addition, tests with different contaminated and uncontaminated MTVs are presented where the difference in absorbance at different wavelengths is observed. This work is my final degree work, which won first place in the category of Health Sciences Engineering for the Jornada de Iniciación Cientifica at the Universidad Tecnológica de Panamá.
