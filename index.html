3-Players Reflex Game

The project consists of a mockup which has an Arduino MEGA, 4 buttons, 4 LEDs, an LCD screen with I2C, and a buzzer. The game is based on testing the reflexes of the players, there is a "boss" who presses a button, which activates a green LED that turns on between 1 to 10 seconds, at the moment this LED turns off, the players must press their button which will activate its LED (red, yellow or white), the fastest to press the button wins, there are 3 rounds and the best of 3 wins. At the end the LCD will display the winner and points. Click the title for more information!
