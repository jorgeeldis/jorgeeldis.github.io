Stoa Calculator

This project is about a calculator for all the students in a STEM career, it has from basic arithmetic to integrals, from changing binary numbers to octals, & much more! It's available on pip3 for python: pip3 install stoacalculator

RoboStoa

RoboStoa is an AI robot, powered by arduino, it has a bluetooth speaker integrated and a 7 Segment LED screen, it's the first version, but still needs a lot of upgrades, like some wheels, a special program that can answer to your voice, & much more.

Stoa Community

Stoa Community is a community i created, the goal of this community is to bring the programmers, engineers, physicist, and all those who work in the STEM field, with news, projects, posts, books, papers, games and so much more!

WhiteCode

WhiteCode is an AI Chatbot created with Python, this chatbot was my first real project i made with python and it's on standby, my goal is to take this AI and merge the software of RoboStoa with WhiteCode and then have a complete AI Robot.
